## Scoring Definitions

Probability and impact are rated on a 1-5 scale. Score = Probability x Impact.

| Score | Probability    | Impact     |
|-------|----------------|------------|
| 1     | Rare           | Negligible |
| 2     | Unlikely       | Minor      |
| 3     | Possible       | Moderate   |
| 4     | Likely         | Major      |
| 5     | Almost certain | Severe     |

Level bands: 1-6 Low, 8-12 Medium, 15-16 High, 20-25 Critical.
