# Meeting Minutes

## Purpose

> **Scope:** Formal records of what a meeting decided and assigned.
>
> **Key items:** Attendees with roles, agenda-driven body, decisions, action
> items with one owner each.

## When To Use

Use for meeting minutes and published agendas. PMBOK lists meetings as a
project management method and each meeting requires both an agenda and
minutes.

For informal scratch notes use `types/quick-note.md` instead.

**Templates:**

- `templates/en/meeting-minutes-template-en.md`
- `templates/pl/meeting-minutes-template-pl.md`

## Structure

1. H1 title carrying the meeting name - `# Steering Committee - 2026-09-20`.
2. Meeting details block - date, time, location or platform, facilitator,
   minute taker.
3. Attendees - each with their role, plus the absentees.
4. Agenda - a table of topics and their owners.
5. Discussion - per agenda item, a short summary ending with the outcome.
6. Decisions - a table with decision, rationale, owner, and date.
7. Action Items - a table with task, one owner, due date, and status.
8. Open Questions - unresolved items and who chases them.
9. Next Meeting - date and time.

## Deltas From The Language Baseline

- Record what was done, not what was said. Minutes are never a transcript.
- Each action item carries exactly one owner and a due date.
- Decisions are specific enough to act on without the meeting context.
- The agenda is the pre-meeting draft of the same document. Publish it, then
  fill in the outcomes.
- Carried-forward action items keep their owner. Note date changes, do not
  silently renumber or reassign.
- Placement - conventionally `docs/minutes/` with date-named files, for
  example `2026-09-20-steering.md`.
