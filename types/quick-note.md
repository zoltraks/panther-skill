# Note

## Purpose

> **Scope:** Conventions for quick notes - short working documents, outlines, meeting notes, and
> drafts that capture content fast
> **Key items:** minimal structure, optional H1, informal lists, tolerance for loose conventions

A note is the lightest document type.

It captures information quickly and is allowed to stay informal.

Typical shape: a short outline that may start at H2, use `*` bullets or lettered subsections,
and stay informal.

## When To Use

Use for quick notes, drafts, outlines, scratch documentation, and any short document where speed
matters more than ceremony.

For formal meeting records with decisions and action items use `types/meeting-minutes.md`
instead.

**Templates**

- `templates/en/quick-note-template-en.md`
- `templates/pl/quick-note-template-pl.md`

## Structure

A note may start directly at H2 with no H1 title.

Sections are optional - a note may be a single list.

Subsections may use letters (`### A. Topic`) or plain names.

## Deltas From The Language Baseline

- The H1 title and purpose section are optional for notes.
- `*` bullets are acceptable when the existing note already uses them, use `-` for new notes.
- Minimal formatting is fine, but encoding, trailing-whitespace, and character rules still apply.
- When a note graduates into a real document, reformat it to the matching type and the full
  language rules at that moment - not before.

## Section Names

No fixed vocabulary - keep whatever names the author used, matching the language of the note.
