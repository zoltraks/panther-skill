# Construction Glossary (Polish-German)

## Purpose

> **Scope:** Polish-German terminology for construction, civil engineering, and
> concrete-durability subjects
> **Key items:** domain signals, terminology table, context forms, calque traps,
> untranslated terms

Load this glossary for translate tasks on construction and building-materials subjects.

Apply it for Polish to German translate tasks, per `process/translate-document.md`.

## Domain Signals

Apply this glossary when:

- The request names a construction, civil-engineering, or building-materials subject.
- The document covers exposure classes, corrosion processes, reinforcement, durability,
  or concrete structures.
- The document's terminology is dominated by material-degradation vocabulary -
  karbonatyzacja, chlorki, cykle zamarzania, atak siarczanowy, ścieranie.

## Context Forms

Some Polish terms render differently by context - do not force one form everywhere:

- `atak` renders `Angriff` in process names (`atak chlorkowy` - `Chloridangriff`), while
  `oddziaływanie` in formal class naming renders `Einwirkung` - `oddziaływanie chlorków`
  becomes `chloridhaltige Einwirkung`, not `Chloridangriff`.
- `rysowanie` renders `Rissbildung` for fine surface cracking - `pękanie` renders
  `Rissbildung` as well, and `Riss` for the crack itself.
- `otulina` renders `Betondeckung` - never `Betonabdeckung`.
- `zbrojenie` renders `Bewehrung` - `Stahlbewehrung` when steel must be explicit.
- `zużycie` renders `Verschleiß` for mechanical wear - `Verbrauch` only for resource
  consumption.
- `nieuszkodzony` and `nienaruszony` both render `unversehrt` or `intakt`.
- `zgodność` renders `Konformität` in standards contexts.
- `posadzka` renders `Estrich` - `Hartstoffestrich` for hard-aggregate screeds under
  XM3 per DIN 1100.

## Untranslated

Settled terms stay in their canonical form in German output:

- Exposure-class codes `X0`, `XC`, `XD`, `XS`, `XF`, `XA`, `XM` and their subdivisions
  (`XC1`-`XC4`, `XD1`-`XD3`, `XS1`-`XS3`, `XF1`-`XF4`, `XA1`-`XA3`, `XM1`-`XM3`) stay
  verbatim.
- Chemical formulas and ions `CO₂`, `Ca(OH)₂`, `CaCO₃`, `H₂O`, `NaCl`, `MgCl₂`,
  `SO₄²⁻`, `H⁺`, `Cl⁻` stay verbatim.
- Strength classes `C20/25`, `C35/45` stay verbatim.
- `pH`, `√t`, and the formula `x_c(t) ∝ √t` stay verbatim.
- Standard names `PN-EN 206`, `DIN EN 206`, `DIN 1045-2`, `DIN EN 1992-1-1`
  (`Eurocode 2`), `DIN 4030-1`, `DIN 1100` stay verbatim - `DIN` designations are the
  canonical German forms.
